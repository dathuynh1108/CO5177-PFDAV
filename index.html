02 Selected assignments

Three perspectives.
One curious mindset.

Tabular and Text are our two required tracks. Image completes the collection as our selected elective.

ASSIGNMENT / 01Required

Tabular

Find the story in the structure.

Explore patterns, handle missing values and build models from numerical and categorical features.

≥ 2,000 samples · ≥ 10 columns

03 A shared approach

From first question
to a clear conclusion.

Each assignment follows a reproducible workflow, with a separate notebook that runs end to end on Google Colab.

Step 01

Explore

Understand the source, ask questions and visualize the data.

Step 02

Prepare

Clean, transform and split the data without information leakage.

Step 03

Model

Build baselines and compare models using consistent evaluation.

Step 04

Explain

Connect metrics to findings, limitations and possible extensions.
