Study overview

The study classifies Vietnamese news articles into 19 VietnamNet categories. PhoBERT Base v2 and its classification head are fine-tuned on article text. Temperature scaling adjusts confidence, and class-specific thresholds adjust the final category decision.

Dataset

VietnamNet · 212,705 articles · 19 categories

The full training notebook uses 180,799 training articles and a 31,906-article evaluation split. Inputs combine the headline with the beginning and end of the article body.

The notebook records an April 2026 Kaggle run on two Tesla T4 GPUs. The coursework reuses that run and its saved figures. A separate headline-only baseline study remains available.

Training pipeline

  1. Load and inspect. Read all 19 parquet files in batches. Inspect class imbalance, missing fields and text lengths.
  2. Prepare article text. Combine the headline with the first 220 and last 80 body words. Lowercase, remove punctuation and digits, then segment with PyVi. Stopwords are retained.
  3. Build the dataset. Make a stratified 85/15 split with seed 42. Head–Tail tokenization retains the first 127 and last 127 subwords plus special tokens, with a maximum length of 256.
  4. Fine-tune PhoBERT. Update the encoder and 19-class head using weighted cross-entropy, balanced class weights and the notebook’s fixed class boosts. The run uses seven epochs, AdamW at 2e-5, FP16 and an effective batch of 64.
  5. Evaluate and export. Select the checkpoint by evaluation macro-F1; save the model, tokenizer, label mapping, class report and confusion matrix.
  6. Calibrate and diagnose. Fit a temperature, search per-class thresholds, then inspect F1 changes, precision/recall trade-offs and confusion pairs.

Temperature and class thresholds

A scalar temperature of 1.4583 is fitted by minimizing negative log-likelihood. On the calibration data, printed NLL changes from 0.3828 to 0.3237 and 15-bin ECE from 0.0608 to 0.0218. Temperature alone preserves the predicted category.

The notebook then searches 200 threshold values from 0.3 to 3.5 for each class over three passes. Predictions use argmax(softmax(logits / T) / thresholds). These ratios are decision scores; the model still predicts one category per article.

Accuracy increases by 0.34 percentage points after threshold adjustment. Pháp luật recall increases, while its precision falls; the report includes the complete 19-class table and discusses the corresponding trade-offs.
