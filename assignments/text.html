About the study

This study classifies Vietnamese news articles into 19 categories. It compares PhoBERT fine-tuned on the headline and article body with a headline-only baseline evaluated on the same article IDs.

Data collection

Huỳnh Thành Đạt crawled the corpus from a list of public VietnamNet article URLs. The published dataset records the source URL, category, headline and body. The notebook uses the checksum-verified Parquet snapshot so that the same articles can be used in each run.

VietnamNet · 7,600 sampled articles · 19 categories

The cohort has 400 articles per category. It is divided into 5,320 training, 1,140 validation and 1,140 independent test articles.

Article preparation and fine-tuning

The input combines the headline with up to 220 words from the beginning and 80 from the end of the body. PyVi segments Vietnamese words. Head–Tail tokenization retains information from both ends within PhoBERT's 256-token input.

PhoBERT Base v2 is fine-tuned for four epochs. Validation macro-F1 selects the checkpoint. The same validation partition determines the temperature and class thresholds before final evaluation on the independent test set.

What calibration changes

Temperature scaling reduced validation negative log-likelihood from 0.9739 to 0.6811. Applying the validation-fitted class thresholds reduced held-out accuracy from 83.77% to 83.25%. A better fit to validation confidence scores can coexist with weaker classification decisions on test.
