Assignment 02 · Required

Vietnamese news classification

Word and character TF-IDF classifiers compared with frozen PhoBERT features on VietnamNet headlines.

Measured studyText classification

7,600 headlines · 19 categories · 4 models. Notebook and report are available; video is pending.

Study overview

The task predicts a news category from its Vietnamese headline. The corpus was collected by Huỳnh Thành Đạt and is versioned on Hugging Face. The study compares word TF-IDF, character TF-IDF and fixed PhoBERT representations on the same inputs and split.

Dataset

VietnamNet · 212,705 source articles · 19 categories

A fixed cohort of 7,600 headlines is selected after a full-corpus quality audit. Each category contributes 280 training, 60 validation and 60 test headlines.

Method

  1. Corpus audit. All 212,705 source rows are inspected. Empty headlines and connected groups with duplicate URLs/headlines or conflicting categories are removed before sampling.
  2. Modeling cohort. A deterministic cohort of 400 headlines per category is selected. URLs, article bodies and category names are excluded from model inputs.
  3. Representations. PyVi segments the text. TF-IDF vocabulary and IDF fit train only. PhoBERT remains frozen; masked mean pooling produces 768-dimensional vectors.
  4. Evaluation. Classifier C is selected by validation macro-F1. All four models are evaluated on the same 1,140 test headlines.

Held-out results

One fixed stratified split. Classifier settings are selected using validation macro-F1.
ModelAccuracyMacro-F1
Majority baseline5.26%0.0053
Word TF-IDF + Linear SVM66.32%0.6582
Character TF-IDF + Linear SVM67.28%0.6676
Frozen PhoBERT + Logistic regression77.54%0.7738

Frozen PhoBERT with logistic regression reaches 77.54% test accuracy and 0.7738 macro-F1. Character TF-IDF reaches 67.28% accuracy. The 7,600-headline cohort is balanced; these scores do not describe full-corpus training or classification of complete articles.

Full settings, data checks and limitations are documented in the report and experiment protocol.
