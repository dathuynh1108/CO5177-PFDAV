About the study

This study classifies Vietnamese news articles into 19 categories. It compares PhoBERT fine-tuned on the headline and article body with a headline-only baseline evaluated on the same article IDs.

Dataset source

The corpus is published in the Hugging Face dataset repository of student Huỳnh Thành Đạt. The published dataset records the source URL, category, headline and body. The notebook uses the checksum-verified Parquet snapshot so that the same articles can be used in each run.

VietnamNet · 207,375 usable articles · 19 categories

All 207,375 usable articles are retained after quality checks. Grouped splits contain 145,162 training, 31,107 validation and 31,106 independent test articles. Articles sharing a final headline or Head–Tail token input stay in the same partition.

Article preparation and fine-tuning

The input combines the headline with up to 220 words from the beginning and 80 from the end of the body. PyVi segments Vietnamese words. Head–Tail tokenization retains information from both ends within PhoBERT's 256-token input.

PhoBERT Base v2 completes seven fine-tuning epochs. Validation macro-F1 selects the epoch-seven checkpoint. The same validation partition determines the temperature and class thresholds before final evaluation on the independent test set.

What calibration changes

Temperature scaling reduced validation negative log-likelihood from 0.3781 to 0.3098. With validation-fitted class thresholds, held-out accuracy increased from 91.84% to 91.89%, while macro-F1 increased from 0.9109 to 0.9114. The gain in classification metrics is small relative to the improvement over the headline baselines.
